Lecture series

Frontiers of Mathematics and Computing.

Frontiers of Mathematics and Computing is a lecture series organized jointly by the Department of Mathematics and the newly formed College of Connected Computing at Vanderbilt.

This year the series focuses on AI and mathematics. Beyond the lecture, every speaker also holds office hours for students who want to talk with them; times for both will be announced closer to each date.
